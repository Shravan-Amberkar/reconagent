ReconAgentMonth-end reconciliation for fee-collecting organisations

Who paid? Who didn't?
And where did the money go?

A school, clinic or housing society collects fees through Razorpay, UPI and bank transfers, but the bank statement shows one lump credit per settlement, after fees, GST, refunds and chargebacks have been taken out. Parents pay without a reference, siblings are paid in one go, and a bank holiday shifts everything by a day. ReconAgent matches every bank credit to its Razorpay settlement and every payment to the right invoice. Rules clear the easy cases, an AI agent investigates the rest, and a verifier re-checks every match with exact arithmetic. Anything it can't prove comes to you.
